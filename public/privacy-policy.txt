1. INTRODUCTION
Welcome to Readapt ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your 
privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is handled. 

This Privacy Policy explains how Readapt collects, uses, stores, and protects 
your personal information when you use our mobile application ("App") and 
associated services.

By using Readapt, you agree to the collection and use of information in 
accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, 
please do not use the application.


2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We only collect information necessary to provide, personalize, and improve 
your learning and reading experience.

A. Personal Information (Provided by You)
- Account Credentials: When you sign in (e.g., via Google OAuth or Email), 
  we receive basic profile details such as your Name, Email Address, and 
  Profile Picture URL.
- Account ID: A unique identifier assigned to your account to securely sync 
  your data across devices.

B. App Activity and User-Generated Content
- Reading & Learning Progress: Current chapter read, reading history, streak 
  counters, last active dates, and completed content.
- User Interactions: Bookmarks, highlights, notes/annotations, liked content, 
  and quiz/exercise responses.
- Offline Downloads: Content cached locally on your device storage to allow 
  offline reading and audio playback.

C. Financial and Payment Information
- READAPT DOES NOT COLLECT, PROCESS, OR STORE ANY FINANCIAL DATA.
- We do not ask for or collect credit card numbers, debit card details, bank 
  account information, or UPI credentials.
- All digital features during the Early Access period are provided completely 
  free of charge. Any future in-app purchases or subscriptions will be processed 
  exclusively and securely through Google Play In-App Billing in full compliance 
  with Google Play Policies.

D. Device and Diagnostic Information
- Device Information: Operating system version, device model, and language 
  preference to ensure proper rendering and responsive layout.
- Crash Reports & Performance: Anonymous error logs and performance diagnostics 
  to help us identify and fix software bugs.


3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
- To authenticate and maintain your user account.
- To synchronize your reading progress, streaks, bookmarks, and notes across sessions.
- To deliver streaming audio narration and synchronized reading material.
- To provide offline access to requested chapters and books.
- To detect, prevent, and address technical bugs, security issues, or abuse.
- To communicate critical service updates or respond to your customer support requests.


4. DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE
We respect your privacy. WE DO NOT SELL, RENT, OR TRADE YOUR PERSONAL DATA 
TO THIRD PARTIES FOR ADVERTISING OR MARKETING PURPOSES.

We share data only with trusted service infrastructure providers strictly 
necessary to run the App:
- Cloud Hosting & Database: MongoDB Atlas / Cloud providers for secure encrypted data storage.
- Authentication: Google Identity Services for secure login authentication.
- Media Storage: Secure cloud storage (Backblaze B2 / CDN) for serving educational book covers, audio, and PDF materials.

All third-party partners are bound by strict data protection agreements and 
industry-standard security practices.


5. DATA SECURITY & ENCRYPTION
- Data in Transit: All data transferred between the Readapt App and our servers 
  is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.2+).
- Data at Rest: User credentials, tokens, and database records are stored in 
  secured, access-restricted databases with encryption-at-rest.
- Offline Data: Downloaded reading files stored on your local device are isolated 
  within the application's private app sandbox.


6. DATA RETENTION & ACCOUNT DELETION POLICY
We retain your personal data only as long as your account remains active.

You have the complete right to request the deletion of your account and all 
associated personal data at any time:

A. In-App Account Deletion:
- Open the Readapt App -> Go to the Profile Tab -> Tap "Delete Account" -> 
  Confirm in the dialog.

B. Web-Based Account Deletion (Without Installing the App):
- Visit our public Deletion Portal at:
  https://redapt-admin.vercel.app/delete-account
- Enter your registered email address to check your status or request deletion.

C. 48-Hour Grace Period & Recovery:
- To protect you against accidental deletions, deletion requests are placed in a 
  48-hour grace period.
- You can cancel the deletion request at any time during these 48 hours simply by:
  (1) Logging back into the Readapt App, OR
  (2) Visiting the Deletion Portal and tapping "Cancel Deletion Request".

D. What Data is Deleted vs. Anonymized:
- Permanently Purged: Name, email address, profile photo, authentication tokens, 
  reading history, streak counters, bookmarks, and quiz scores will be permanently 
  deleted from active servers after the 48-hour grace period.
- Anonymized Content: In accordance with Google Play Policy and community integrity, 
  public discussion comments made by the user will be unlinked and anonymized 
  (attributed to "Anonymous / Deleted User") to avoid breaking public conversation threads.


7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Readapt is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly 
collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover 
that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately 
delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child 
has provided personal data, please contact us.
